# Wellness dashboard prompt: Routines & Beauty

Paste this into Claude to build a personal wellness page. Fill in the bracketed parts first.
Works best in Claude with artifacts turned on, so the page can save your data.

---

Build me a one-page wellness dashboard called **Routines & Beauty**. It should save everything
I enter (use the artifact's database if you can, otherwise browser storage) and look calm and
premium: dark glass panels, soft color, readable on my phone.

**About me:** [age range, goals, e.g. "better posture, glowing skin, glute and core strength,
healthy hair"]. My week: [e.g. train Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri/Sat, wash hair Mon/Thu].

## 1. Today, as routines I tick off in one tap

Group my habits into **Morning, Shower, Evening, Anytime**. Each group shows as one row with a
single check: ticking the group ticks every habit in it, and I can open it to tick one at a time.

Each habit has:
- name, group, and which days of the week it's on (e.g. retinol Tue/Fri only)
- optional **how-to**: a follow-along video link, numbered steps, and products

Tapping a habit opens a how-to sheet with "Watch the video" plus the steps and products, so
when I tap "Face massage" I can follow along right there.

Start me with these: [list your habits, e.g. "Make bed, Morning sunlight, Vitamins, Stretches, AM skincare, Face
massage; Shower: dry brush M/W/F/Sat, hair wash Mon/Thu, body wash, body scrub Tue/Sat,
body oil; Evening: PM skincare, retinal Tue/Fri, body retinol Wed/Sun, AHA lotion Mon/Thu,
posture opener; Anytime: water, protein, training, steps, deep core"]. Rules: never put
exfoliating steps (scrub, AHA, retinol) on the same night.

## 2. A weekly tracker

A 7-day grid of every habit with checkmarks, so I can see streaks and gaps.

## 3. Beauty calendar

- **Recurring services** (hair, nails, brows, lashes, facials) with how often I go and who I see.
- **Want to try** list (e.g. lash lift, a new facial) with notes and links.
- **Log** of what I had done and when ("hair done Oct 3").
- **Upcoming appointments.** When I add one, offer to put it on my Google Calendar.
- From the log and the intervals, show when each service is next due.

## 4. Programs from videos I save

Leave room for programs built from videos: posture, glutes, deep core, hair, face. When the same
move or step shows up in 3+ of my saved videos, mark it with ⭐ in the steps.

Keep the copy short and plain. Don't make medical claims; for anything that hurts or worries
me, the page should say to check with a professional.
